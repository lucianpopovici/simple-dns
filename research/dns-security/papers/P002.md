# P002 — Understanding the Role of Registrars in DNSSEC Deployment

- Type/credibility: peer-reviewed
- Subtopic: dnssec
- Source: ACM IMC, 2017 · https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3131365.3131373
- Status: have-meta · summary: verified

**Research question:** Why is DNSSEC signing adoption so low, and what role do registrars play?
**Method:** Measurement study testing registrar DNSSEC support and the DS-record upload workflows they expose to customers.
**Main findings:** A handful of registrars account for most signed domains; many leading registrars do not support DNSSEC at all or require cumbersome manual steps; default-on signing is rare.
**Key evidence:** Per-registrar feature testing combined with domain signing data.
**Limitations:** Snapshot in time; registrar behaviour and offerings change.
**Relevance to this project:** Why DNSSEC signing adoption stalls — registrar incentives
