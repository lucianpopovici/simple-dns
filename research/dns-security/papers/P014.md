# P014 — Clouding up the Internet: how centralized is DNS traffic becoming?

- Type/credibility: peer-reviewed
- Subtopic: measurement
- Source: ACM IMC, 2020 · https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3419394.3423625
- Status: have-meta · summary: verified

**Research question:** How centralized is DNS traffic becoming toward a few large cloud providers?
**Method:** Three-year (2018-2020) analysis of authoritative traffic at .nl, .nz, and B-Root — 55.7 billion queries.
**Main findings:** More than 30% of queries to both ccTLDs originate from just 5 cloud providers (Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Cloudflare, Facebook); concentration is rising.
**Key evidence:** Authoritative-side query logs across three zones.
**Limitations:** Specific zones; query-side resolver/proxy effects can skew attribution.
**Relevance to this project:** 30%+ of ccTLD queries from 5 cloud providers — centralization
