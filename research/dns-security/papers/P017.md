# P017 — A Second Look at DNS QNAME Minimization

- Type/credibility: peer-reviewed
- Subtopic: encrypted-dns
- Source: PAM, 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-28486-1_21
- Status: have-meta · summary: verified

**Research question:** How widely and correctly is QNAME minimization deployed, and what are its effects?
**Method:** Active/passive measurement of resolver QNAME-minimization behaviour in the wild.
**Main findings:** Adoption grew after RFC standardization, but implementations vary (full vs relaxed minimization) and some add resolution overhead.
**Key evidence:** Resolver behaviour measurements.
**Limitations:** Vantage and resolver-sampling limits.
**Relevance to this project:** Measures real-world QNAME-minimization adoption/behavior
