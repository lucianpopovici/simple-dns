# P023 — TsuNAME: exploiting misconfiguration and vulnerability to DDoS DNS

- Type/credibility: peer-reviewed
- Subtopic: measurement
- Source: ACM IMC, 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3487552.3487824
- Status: have-meta · summary: verified

**Research question:** Can cyclic NS dependencies (misconfiguration) be exploited to DDoS authoritative servers?
**Method:** Measurement of ~184M domains across 7 large TLDs, resolver-behaviour analysis, and analysis of a real-world event.
**Main findings:** Found 44 cyclic-dependent NS records used by ~1.4K domains and 3.6K vulnerable resolvers across 2.6K ASes; affected resolvers loop and amplify queries (one ccTLD saw ~10x traffic). Mitigation: loop detection + caching.
**Key evidence:** Large-scale measurement + observed operational event.
**Limitations:** Impact depends on resolver implementation/caching behaviour.
**Relevance to this project:** Cyclic NS dependencies amplify resolver queries into authoritative DoS
