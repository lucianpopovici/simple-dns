# P001 — A Longitudinal, End-to-End View of the DNSSEC Ecosystem

- Type/credibility: peer-reviewed
- Subtopic: dnssec
- Source: USENIX Security, 2017 · https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity17/technical-sessions/presentation/chung
- Status: have-meta · summary: verified

**Research question:** How well is DNSSEC's PKI actually managed end-to-end, from domain/registrar signing through resolver validation?
**Method:** 21-month longitudinal measurement of all DNSSEC-enabled second-level domains under .com/.org/.net, plus active probing of 59K+ resolvers worldwide for validation behaviour.
**Main findings:** Signing adoption is very low and often misconfigured (missing/incorrect DS records, broken chains of trust); only a minority of resolvers actually validate; registrars are a key bottleneck in the chain.
**Key evidence:** TLD zone + DS records tracked over time; large-scale resolver validation probing.
**Limitations:** gTLD-focused (.com/.org/.net); resolver sample not exhaustive; limited view of client-side validation.
**Relevance to this project:** First large-scale longitudinal view of DNSSEC PKI management + resolver validation
