# P005 — Encrypted DNS => Privacy? A Traffic Analysis Perspective

- Type/credibility: peer-reviewed
- Subtopic: encrypted-dns
- Source: NDSS, 2020 · https://www.ndss-symposium.org/ndss-paper/encrypted-dns-privacy-a-traffic-analysis-perspective/
- Status: have-meta · summary: verified

**Research question:** Does encrypting DNS (DoH/DoT) actually protect users from traffic-analysis-based monitoring and censorship?
**Method:** Machine-learning traffic-analysis classifier applied to encrypted-DNS traces in closed- and open-world settings; evaluates standardized EDNS(0) padding.
**Main findings:** Visited domains are identifiable with high accuracy using ~124x less data than analogous attacks on HTTPS flows; standardized padding schemes are not effective defenses.
**Key evidence:** Classification accuracy on collected DoH/DoT traffic.
**Limitations:** Largely lab/closed-world conditions; tied to specific resolvers and capture period.
**Relevance to this project:** Shows DoH padding schemes fail against traffic analysis
