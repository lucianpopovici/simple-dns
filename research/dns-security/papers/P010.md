# P010 — DNS Cache Poisoning Attack: Resurrections with Side Channels

- Type/credibility: peer-reviewed
- Subtopic: cache-poisoning
- Source: ACM CCS, 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3460120.3486219
- Status: have-meta · summary: verified

**Research question:** Do SAD DNS-style side channels persist after initial patches, and are there new variants?
**Method:** Identification of additional network side channels and re-evaluation of resolver/OS exposure.
**Main findings:** New side channels re-enable the attack; initial patches were incomplete and a broader set of systems remains exposed.
**Key evidence:** New PoCs and re-measurement.
**Limitations:** Same attack class; mitigations continue to evolve.
**Relevance to this project:** Follow-up SAD DNS variants; broader OS exposure
