# P015 — Measuring Centralization of DNS Infrastructure in the Wild

- Type/credibility: peer-reviewed
- Subtopic: measurement
- Source: AINA (Springer), 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-44041-1_76
- Status: have-meta · summary: verified

**Research question:** How concentrated is authoritative DNS infrastructure through shared third-party hosting?
**Method:** Active measurement (the 'dnstracker' tool) over the authoritative name servers of the Alexa top 1M.
**Main findings:** Up to ~12,000 authoritative name servers share the underlying infrastructure of a single third-party provider — a significant single-point-of-failure/concentration risk.
**Key evidence:** Active resolution + infrastructure mapping.
**Limitations:** Active-measurement vantage; Alexa-list representativeness issues.
**Relevance to this project:** dnstracker tool; shared authoritative infra concentration
